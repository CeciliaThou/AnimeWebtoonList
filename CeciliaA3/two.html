Favourites List

Welcome to the Favourite List page!

I watch and read a lot of romance and fantasy, which may have been very evident in my previous lists. But those aren’t the only genres in this list. There may also be ones about food or slice of life. It was quite hard for me to come up with a list since there's been so many good watches and reads over the course of my life.

Favourites List

  • Shadows House
  • Who Made Me a Princess
  • The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity
  • Lady Chef Royale

Shadows House

This anime is about the life of Kate the Shadow and Emilico the “Living Doll”. In the Shadows house, the Shadows are noble children and the Living Dolls are there to act and serve as the Shadows face. They also clean the soot that is emitted by every Shadow. One of my favourite parts about this anime is the ending song called Nai Nai.

Who Made Me a Princess

I feel like I need to add this one to the list. Not only was it the first webtoon I read, but the artstyle is also just really pretty. This story is about a girl who reincarnates into a character in a novel. That character is Athanasia, the princess who is fated to be killed by her father, the emperor. Now that she has become Athanasia she needs to plan for the future to prevent her death. Will she survive? Although this webtoon did end, they are releasing side stories. They also got an anime adaptation.

The Fragrant Flower Blooms with Dignity

This is a school romance story between two students from opposing schools. Rintaro from the delinquent boys school and Kaoruko from the elite girls school. The romance between the two blossoms despite all the prejudice the schools have with one another, and it all started from a simple interaction at a bakery.

Lady Chef Royale

Sena, the main character, finds herself in a different world as the hated daughter of a noble family. In her previous life she ran a little restaurant. To find comfort in this world, she continues to cook in this new environment. This romance fantasy cooking webtoon currently has 166 chapters. I would say chapter #23: Be Prepared is my favourite chapter because it includes a very satisfying revenge plot.
