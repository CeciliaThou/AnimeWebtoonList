Current Watch/Read List

This webpage would be extremely long if I had the full list. Each anime or webtoon will include a short summary written by me which will not include any spoilers. Or at the very least, anything that is said you will learn either in the first episode or first couple of chapters.

Always a Catch!, The Fish I Missed Was Big

This anime is about Maria, the daughter of a duke, who is looking for eligible bachelors to marry. By the end of the first episode, she is mistaken for her best friend, Aida, and ends up arguing with the soon to be crown prince of the kingdom. This anime is based off of a light-novel, and at the time of writing has one episode available.

Agents of the Four Seasons: Dance of Spring

This anime also only has one episode out so far. It is a fantasy anime about how the 4 seasons, winter, spring, summer, and fall, transition from one to another. The main character Hinagiku is the agent of spring and in the first episode, she returns after a 10 year wait to change winter to spring.

The Food Diary of Miss Maid

The main character Suzume will be living in Japan for one year. During that year she decides to try as many foods as possible. Whether its sweets, snacks, or street food, she’ll be tasting, rating, and documenting her year long food journey. I really like the ending of the episode where you can watch a timelapse of Suzune being drawn.

Kaya-chan Isn't Scary

Kaya-chan is a troublemaker with a little secret. This story takes place at a kindergarten in Japan. This action-horror anime has 12 episodes and recently ended on March 29 of this year.

I’m a Villaness, but I Won’t Live Like One

This fantasy romance is about Bianca. One day she finds a book foretelling her whole life and how it will all end. Turns out she is a villainess who is to be killed by her fiance. In order to avoid her death, she attempts to change her fate and fix all the problems told in the book. There are currently 60 chapters.

The Price is Your Everything

This fantasy drama is about a girl named Nerys who has lived an unfortunate and cruel life, being bullied, manipulated, used, and tossed aside. After her death she gets taken back to a time right before all of her misfortunes started. Will she be able to change her fate, or will she relive her previous life? There are currently 110 chapters.

Aiming for the Alimony

Reincarnated as Ibelin, a character in a novel who is destined to die, she decides to try to change her destiny by divorcing her arranged husband. Will she succeed and survive her fate? This romance fantasy has 58 chapters so far.

Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint (ORV)

ORV is a story about Kim Dokja. Dokja was reading an extremely long fantasy survival novel, which has over 1000 chapters, when all of a sudden it became reality. Using the knowledge he gained from being one of the only people to read the novel till the end, he survives each of the trials, meets the novel characters, and changes the fate of not just him, but others as well. Just like the novel Dokja was reading, this webtoon has quite a lot of chapters as well. It has 305 chapters at the moment.

Can I Finally Cry Now

This story is about an academically gifted little girl named Ivy. Although she is a commoner, she is able to get into a prestigious noble school through hard work and dedication by passing the entrance exam. There she becomes friends with some of the most powerful noble children, and meets some unique professors. This is a very cute but also sad story and currently has 34 chapters.
